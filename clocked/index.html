Pebble Games

Clocked

Games
  • Tap spots to queue moves, then press Tick to make them all at once.
  • A spot can change only when the spot before it has a pebble that stays put this tick.
  • A pebble you lift is back in your hand only after the tick.
  • Goal: both steps below, in as few ticks as you can.
