Reversible Erasure Prices Pits Spooky DAG Red-blue Clocked
  • A tap places or removes a pebble, only when the spot before has a pebble.
  • Hold a pebble to turn it into a ghost. That frees the pebble, on any spot.
  • To clear a ghost, tap it to pebble it again, then remove that pebble.
  • Goal: both steps below, in as few moves as you can.

Why this game?

A quantum computer can measure a qubit away instead of uncomputing it, leaving a phase to fix later. That trades qubits for time.
