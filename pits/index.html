  • You only have the pebbles shown below the board.
  • A pit takes all its pebbles at once, one per slot.
  • Tap a pit to fill it or empty it. Each tap is one move.
  • You can only tap a pit when the pit before it is full.
  • Goal: both steps below, in as few moves as you can.
