Why this game?

A reversible computer can't throw scratch work away, since erasing costs heat. It has to finish clean, uncomputing everything but the answer, and that cleanup costs extra moves or extra memory.

Bennett, SIAM J. Comput. 1989 · Knill, arXiv:math/9508218
