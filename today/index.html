Why this game?

On today's chips, moving data costs far more than doing math: reading 32 bits from DRAM takes about 640 pJ, a 32-bit multiply about 3.7 pJ. Dropping a value is free; the cost is in writing it out and reading it back, or computing it again. Keeping values on the chip between steps is how FlashAttention beats unfused attention.

Prices: moving one slot costs 2, a matrix multiply about as much as reading its weights, an element-wise step 1.
